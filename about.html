About Bethesda

Our Story & Values

Bethesda Group of Schools, Yaoundé

Bethesda takes its name from the biblical pool of Bethesda — a place of healing and renewal — and carries that same spirit of care into the classroom. We are a Nursery, Primary and Secondary school community in Yaoundé, Cameroon, built around the idea that children learn best when they feel safe, valued and known.

From a child's very first day in Nursery through their final GCE Advanced Level exams in Upper Sixth, Bethesda offers one consistent, caring environment — so families never have to search for a new school as their children grow.

This page is part of a concept design for Bethesda. Exact history, founding details and leadership information should be confirmed directly with the school before publishing as an official site.
